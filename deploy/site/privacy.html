Privacy Policy

MetalArm · last updated September 2026

MetalArm turns your goals, habits and gym workouts into RPG-style progression. This page explains what we store, why, and how to delete it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, display name, time zone and weight-unit preference. Your password is stored only as a salted bcrypt hash.
  • Activity you log: workouts, sets, personal records, body measurements, quests, rewards and points.
  • Party membership: the parties you create or join. Party members can see your display name, level, rank and points in that party.

How we use it

Only to run the app: to sign you in, calculate your points, levels and records, and show party leaderboards. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use third-party tracking or analytics.

Where it is stored

On the MetalArm server over encrypted HTTPS connections. The iOS app keeps your sign-in tokens in the device Keychain.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Encrypted backups are kept for up to 14 days and then removed.

Deleting your account

In the iOS app, go to Profile → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and everything you logged. A party you own is handed to its longest-standing member. You can also email us to request deletion.
