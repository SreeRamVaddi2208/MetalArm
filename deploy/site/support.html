Common questions

I forgot my password. Email us from the address on your account and we will help you back in.

My points look wrong. Points, records and levels are calculated on the server from what you logged. Workouts shorter than 10 minutes or with fewer than 3 working sets earn set points but no completion bonus.

How do I delete my account? In the iOS app: Profile → Delete account. See the privacy policy for what that removes.
